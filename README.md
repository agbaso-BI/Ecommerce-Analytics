# 🛒 E-commerce Store Analysis — Power BI

A 5-page Power BI dashboard analyzing 1,000 orders and $1.20M in revenue across sales performance, customer behavior, feedback and retention, and shipping operations.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop

## 📂 Data Source
Dataset obtained from [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com)

## 💡 Key Insights
- The store generated **$1.20M** in revenue from **1K orders** and **5K units** sold, with **290 customers**
- **Home & Kitchen** is the top category at **$0.37M**, driven by Appliances
- Laptop and Blender lead product revenue at about **$0.20M** each; Toaster is lowest among named products at **$0.17M**
- Payment methods are evenly split: **Bank Transfer 35.19%**, **PayPal 34.53%**, **Credit Card 30.28%**
- Quantity and total sales rise together, while discount shows no clear relationship with total sales
- **85.3%** of customer feedback is neutral, with **7.5%** positive and **7.2%** negative
- Total shipping cost is **$12.42K**; Laptop ($2,113.79) and Headphones ($2,062.83) are the most expensive products to ship
- A large share of orders has no customer ID ("Not Provided"), which limits customer-level analysis
